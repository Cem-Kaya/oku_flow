Readable UCT text could disappear during unrelated scrolling, paused zoom recovery, tilted rendering, or supplemental crop admission. These changes preserve complete crossing sentences and uncertainty masks, retain independently proved other-pane text without renewing its evidence, and carry incoming typography and four-corner geometry through local color/ink fitting and painting.

An opt-in active regional executor now supplements baseline full scans with one bounded current-input crop per interval. Full work keeps queue priority and its existing deadlines; insufficient optional slack yields without inference. Regional outputs never train the shadow planner, invent acquisitions, retire a healthy backend, or replace the paused selected/real-neighbour detail pair. Regional ownership uses the detector's shared eight-pixel crop exclusion plus two pixels of jitter slack and original detector bounds. Rejected overlapping crop fragments cannot delete otherwise owned lines. Wide prose may receive no regional contribution; no full-scan savings are claimed.

Paused navigation reprojects existing text immediately and can request bounded 6x/960-pixel detail after 300 ms. Genuine backend/presentation recovery rearms a lost settled request. Immutable refinement preserves prior typography, uses retained geometry for duplicate carving, and keeps risky/contradictory ink protected. Subpixel carve strips no longer suppress adjacent proved words; tiny uncertain punctuation remains protected.

Native playback repairs keep healthy long-GOP seeks progressing under bounded idle/absolute limits, pace PCM handoff and fence Pause/Resume/EOS ownership. Embedded FFmpeg/dav1d remains an explicit private SDK/environment prototype; Media Foundation stays default and owns archives. No codec runtime is silently deployed.
