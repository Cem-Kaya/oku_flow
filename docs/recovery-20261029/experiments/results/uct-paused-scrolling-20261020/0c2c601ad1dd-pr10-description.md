Saved videos now share the CUDA effects and Ultra Clear Text pipeline, with CPU fallback and source-bound playback, seeking, rotation and frame saving. Select a file through Settings > Camera > View video, Ctrl+O or file drop. Accessible icon actions sit beside the scrubber and time, with a narrow layout fallback.

Recording and capture behavior:
- Opening a saved video finalizes an ongoing recording. Starting a recording from saved playback returns live. Stopping the same recording session preserves its currently viewed position as saved playback.
- Forward targets the current recording's live edge or a saved file's end-and-hold. Resume continues from the held position. Ordinary camera use has no history buffer/timeline.
- New video recordings contain one clean Original stream before hold, image effects, UCT or ink. Processed photos and saved drawings include eligible UCT/ink frozen with the chosen frame; original photos stay clean. Legacy paired recordings and their notes/transcripts remain supported.

UCT retains source/acquisition identity through Temporal Smooth, exact Extra Stable holds and presentation-clock reprojection. Crossfades cannot confirm text. Neural inputs/tensors and GPU reference/color/ink work remain resident; Unicode/geometry publication uses compact CPU metadata. Word-local colors preserve selected yellow buttons alongside dark neighbours. Text Clarity is a master switch that retains the saved UCT preference and fine-tuning. Advanced UCT exposes its actual CPU/GPU backend and changing status accessibly.

The latest repair addresses a paused-video defect where static columns received sharp text but readable scrolling columns stayed original. A bounded neighbour-worker check confirms same-text words at their shifted neighbour locations while leaving glyphs at the selected frame's coordinates. Changed ink and failed worker proof remain refused. Completed line/word receipts are consumed independently of the UI's inline match budget, so dense one-shot results cannot lose later lines to that budget. No additional OCR worker, fabricated acquisition or periodic still-frame read is added.
