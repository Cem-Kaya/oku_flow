## Findings

**P2, medium confidence: budget-limited probe checks have no guaranteed progress past persistently invisible leading lines.** Caller `OverlayModel::LineReferences` sorts invalid lines first, then oldest validation time (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:4024-4037`). `ProbeWorker::State::Run` passes that snapshot to `CheckLines` (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:2610-2615`), which starts at offset zero every request and checks its wall budget only between 64-line resident batches / individual CPU lines (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:1514-1565`). `ApplyChecks` retains invisible lines as invalid without updating their validation timestamps; unchecked lines also retain their timestamps (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:4080-4093`). Counterexample: the leading invisible batch consumes the budget on successive probes; later on-screen lines never get checked and eventually hide. OCR and probes use separate workers, so this is validation-line starvation, not proven OCR-queue starvation. Actual likelihood/cost at the default 60-ms budget is unknown. Falsifier: an unrun scripted resident oracle returning invisible checks while exhausting one batch's budget, followed by a visible sentinel line; show that the sentinel eventually gets checked under unchanged snapshots. A fair bounded continuation cursor could repair progress without treating unchecked lines as valid; ordering would be a policy change.

**P3, high confidence: optional full fallback is reserved using the regional estimate, not a full-call bound.** `Submit/SubmitDevice -> Enqueue -> Run` checks `slack > estimatedMs + 80`, with `estimatedMs >= 750`, after hold resolution/readback (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:2049-2054,2083-2112`). With sufficient regional slack but an unsupported/stale plan, `job.detail` becomes empty and ordinary full recognition starts (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:2119-2138`). Counterexample: a short ROI would fit, but the fallback full call takes longer than remaining slack; a full request arriving after the check waits behind it. Overrun is reported afterwards (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:2302-2315`), not preempted. This qualifies the reservation, not the documented absence of hard real-time guarantees. Falsifier: a scripted unsupported-detail backend with separately controlled full/ROI costs; prove fallback is refused when only ROI slack fits. Consider an explicit optional yield or separately priced full fallback, preserving mandatory baseline submissions and diagnostics.

**P3, high confidence, interpretation limitation:** shadow coverage deadlines are not active-executor coverage guarantees. `Observe` marks all selected items serviced (`src/common/uct_region_planner.cpp:885-939`); `RegionalDetailFocus` executes only the selection nearest current focus (`src/common/uct_region_planner.cpp:344-352`). Constant focus can repeatedly choose one occurrence while other hypothetical selections age as serviced. Full scans still cover the scene. Falsifier: an active-region trace mapping actual ROIs to every shadow selection by the reported observation deadline. Do not present shadow debt as measured regional service.

## Ownership and safety audit

- **Occurrence identity:** geometry-first, deterministic one-to-one greedy pairing over at most eight overlaps per occurrence; text only determines repeated/changed status (`src/common/uct_region_planner.cpp:576-679`). Missing words reset streaks and expire after the observation-age limit; oldest missing entries can also be evicted at capacity (`src/common/uct_region_planner.cpp:682-769`). IDs never reuse across resets and stop minting at exhaustion (`src/common/uct_region_planner.cpp:238-257,740-765`). Coverage quota has oldest-anchor fairness for hypothetical selections, including churn (`src/common/uct_region_planner.cpp:219-235,839-883`); it is not wall-time fairness.
- **Epochs and resets:** all job/acquisition/time monotonicity checks precede automatic source/geometry/authority reset (`src/common/uct_region_planner.cpp:451-495`). Worker authority changes explicitly reset before observation, allowing a backward seek under new authority (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:2343-2353`). Cancellation revokes pending work, clears the active full plan and advances authority (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:2490-2500`). Plan execution additionally requires identical image mapping, processing-history epoch, smoothing mode/alpha, later acquisition and later frame (`src/common/uct_region_planner.cpp:311-329`). Motion is handled by independent checks; the planner itself is neither optical flow nor a motion-reset detector. Source/resize/seek/appearance calls that trigger cancellation belong to specialist A.
- **Queue/full priority:** one running job and one overwritten pending slot (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:1874-1916`). Region cannot replace queued full; full replaces queued region and clears the plan (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:1963-1988`). Worker rechecks queued-full priority immediately before inference; insufficient slack yields without any recognition (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:2091-2136`). Once the native call starts it is not preempted by a later full enqueue. `usedRegionalPlan` prevents a second valid ROI on that full plan, but interval clocks, one controller submission per interval, high-water estimate updates and failure/overrun latches are caller-owned. The service reports the metadata; it does not implement those latches. Required docs specify 350-ms demanded advancing-media minimum, 1500-ms stable refresh, 80-ms probes and max(previous, 2*actual regional duration+80) estimate; A must verify these caller policies. Only successful complete full results observe the planner, never ROI results (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:2335-2353`). Demand-heavy full traffic may intentionally leave zero regional opportunities.
- **Ordering:** this single worker invokes callbacks serially (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:2360-2367`); it cannot finish a region after a subsequently executed full internally. `IsCurrent` checks authority only (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:2511-2514`), and model admission does not independently reject lower job IDs (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:3363-3398`). Queued UI receipt reordering and a regional callback after a newer full submission therefore require A's exact job-ticket fence. A cancel can occur after the worker snapshots `current` but before callback; consumer authority checking remains necessary. No service-only claim of end-to-end ordering is warranted.
- **Held/current pixels:** owned hold metadata is read after its copy event (`src/cuda/uct_analysis_pool.cpp:95-104`), then inconsistent claims become unknown (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:935-945,2049-2071`). `EvidenceAcquisition` resolves held pixels to the original acquisition (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:915-932`). Duplicate held observations do not age the planner; regional execution excludes held inputs. Pending confirmation requires distinct acquisitions, equal authority/scene and, for saved media, unequal same-kind full-image digests (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:3599-3625`). The brief's closed held-probe/Resume finding is not re-reported.
- **Thresholds/ghosts:** preparation requires confidence >=0.7, minimum source word height and ordinary-token eligibility (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:1599-1605`). Temporal OCR needs every candidate word to match recognized-vs-current pixels at NCC 0.94, visibility 0.95, zero search (`include/okuflow/common/ultra_clear_text.hpp:428-430`; `src/cuda/uct_device_analysis.hpp:799-846`). No-candidate/invalid words fail that line. Planner stability demotes on unverified smoothing (`src/common/uct_region_planner.cpp:518-522,660-677`). Model rejects those lines and leaves smoothed admission hidden until independent single-frame checking (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:3529-3553`). Kernel zero radius bypasses translation/refinement (`src/cuda/uct_validation_kernels.cuh:174-178`). NCC is not character-sensitive proof that could justify skipping OCR; the required character-verification document already establishes that limitation.
- **Regional model transaction:** ten-source-pixel interior ownership, whole crossing/outside-line preservation and rejected-overlap no-op precede mutation (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:3406-3467`). Complete proposal must preserve old active words one-to-one and protection; IDs commit only after the transaction (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:3874-3985`). Outside pending observations keep exact individual shared tickets, not the ROI's ticket (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:3994-4006`). Thus changed spelling of an existing owned word normally cannot become an earlier regional correction; baseline full/probes retain correction authority.
- **Bounds/cleanup:** planner caps are constructor-clamped, text sizes checked before content work (`src/common/uct_region_planner.cpp:150-184,408-438`). Regional unions cap lines/words/tokens/masks; pending union caps 8192 (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:3874-3883,3994-4007`). Device preparation caps 1024 lines/8192 words (`src/cuda/uct_device_analysis.hpp:618-646`). Full model admission itself has no equivalent local pending cap; its bound depends on producer output. Probes retain independent integral/sample allocations, not full pool slots (`src/cuda/uct_device_analysis.hpp:573-605,387-404`). Pool copy pairs publish atomically with exact events (`src/cuda/uct_analysis_pool.cpp:427-473`). Unknown completion quarantines owners; fault retention is deliberately not a finite lifetime-memory guarantee.
- **Wakeups/time:** both worker predicates depend only on pending/shutdown, and every insertion/shutdown notifies (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:1988-2010,2423,2578,2670,2700-2705`). Cancel/focus changes do not make that predicate true, so their missing notify is harmless. No periodic OCR-worker sleep exists. Resident fences poll with a two-second steady deadline, initial yields then private timer pauses; hold-copy waits poll/sleep (`src/cuda/uct_device_analysis.hpp:114-130,210-237`; `src/cuda/uct_analysis_pool.cpp:36-58`). These do not pause for media Pause. Worker duration contains recognition, resident preparation and their waits; GPU event intervals overlap host waits and must not be added as independent overhead. Planner time is separate but delays callback. Native CPU fallback stream synchronization has no such cooperative timeout (`src/cuda/uct_analysis_pool.cpp:219`). Source stamp milliseconds are live monotonic request time or selected-media PTS, not universally wall time; full deadlines use `steady_clock`. Native paused sample identity/window uses integer 100-ns PTS separately (`include/okuflow/common/immutable_corroboration.hpp:14-23,46-65`). Advancing admission bypasses the live source-age gate (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:3350-3376`); A owns its wall deadline. Destruction disables callbacks and waits 1500 ms before detach, retaining native inputs (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:2413-2440`).

## Additional latency opportunities after merge/helpers

All ceilings are **unknown**; none is an FPS or native publication claim.

1. **Exact implementation optimization: inter-job overhead.** Worker duration stops before planner, `Done` follows planner, then callback and job destruction precede next dequeue (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:2299,2334-2367`). Trace queued-backlog `Done(i)->Dequeue(i+1)` and separately planner duration; absence of pending work is not scheduler overhead. Reduce only demonstrated callback/copy/destruction work; preserve ordering. Falsifier: no measurable backlog gap.
2. **Exact if identity-bound: duplicate resident setup on the same owned image.** Every `Prepare` allocates/rebuilds its current integral; every saved-media OCR computes its full fingerprint (`src/cuda/uct_device_analysis.hpp:575-605,849-874`), including regional jobs. Cache/share only identical immutable image, device, decimation and provenance, with original completion/quarantine ownership. Distinct acquisitions must remain distinct evidence. Ordinary probes already reuse stored reference samples through `Matches`; there is no per-probe reference recapture to eliminate (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:1530-1536`). Trace same-image setup multiplicity first; zero duplication falsifies benefit. Risk: cross-worker ownership and pool retention.
3. **Exact: planner scratch reuse and stable compaction.** Multiple vectors/sorts and repeated order-preserving removals allocate/move metadata (`src/common/uct_region_planner.cpp:516-630,685-738,809-883`). Reuse bounded scratch; select the same eviction victims then compact once preserving ID order. Measure subphase wall times and exact plan equality. Risk: tie/counter parity; no threshold change.
4. **Exact policy, alternative wait implementation:** measure completed-event-to-observed-ready delay in existing `Finish`/`WaitForCopy` loops before tuning polling (`src/cuda/uct_device_analysis.hpp:210-237`; `src/cuda/uct_analysis_pool.cpp:45-57`). Preserve every fence, cancellation and quarantine path. Already-ready events or negligible observation delay falsify gain.
5. **Approximate scheduling, unchanged proof:** favor wholly owned regions containing previously pending text when estimating useful early delivery. Existing confirmed-word spelling corrections are transactionally refused; genuinely new words still need independent OCR agreement plus current proof (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:3599-3640,3952-3973`). A first changed word observed only regionally waits for the next full to agree. The 750-ms estimate floor remains enforced even after faster calls (`src/common/ultra_clear_text.cpp:2095`), limiting eligibility. Do not lower it speculatively. Trace ROI admission/no-op reasons and first eligible text publication; no earlier admissible words falsifies usefulness. Risk: focus policy, not recognition correctness.
