How Ultra Clear Text actually works

From a real camera or video sample to sharp letters: what is copied, what is recognized, what can be reused, and why the same page may still need another read.

The whole path, in order

UCT is local optical character recognition (OCR) plus a cautious replacement layer. The camera and the viewport keep running on their own clocks. A slow OCR read does not make every displayed frame wait for text.

  1. Acquire real pixels

    A camera acquisition or decoder-issued video sample owns its source identity and time. A held copy remains that same acquisition.

  2. Separate the image paths

    Clean Original serves recording and recap. A geometry-correct, pre-appearance, pre-zoom scene serves UCT. The enhanced scene serves the view.

  3. Choose useful work

    Independent probes check change and freshness. The controller chooses a full read, a paused detail pair, or an optional supplemental region.

  4. Read off the UI thread

    One automatic backend chain runs one recognition job at a time. At most one newer job waits; obsolete waiting work is replaced.

  5. Prepare candidate words

    Detection, recognition, source geometry, local ink colors, reference patches and omission masks travel together.

  6. Require evidence

    Authority, size, confidence, exact-text agreement and independent pixel checks decide what may become a replacement. Agreement can still repeat a wrong word.

  7. Publish separate surfaces

    Reading-pane text, eligible scene words, actual painted glyphs and positioned accessibility text have related but different conditions.

  8. Reproject and recheck

    Pan and zoom map retained words onto the latest successful view. New acquisitions require continued proof; full OCR remains authoritative.

The central distinction: keeping pixels is cheap compared with reading them again, but kept pixels are not new evidence. Keeping an accepted word is useful, but a word that still looks similar may have changed one character. UCT therefore reuses navigation and valid objects while continuing bounded checks and full reads.

01 · What is implemented, optional or proposedRead the status before interpreting the rest of the guide.

Development implementation is not a published download

UCT is experimental and opt-in. Its saved global preference defaults off when absent. In the development app, find it under Advanced → Image → Text clarity → Ultra Clear Text (experimental). The Text Clarity master switch pauses UCT while retaining that preference; switching the master back on restores its use. Show original camera text hides replacement letters for comparison while recognition can continue.

02 · Which pixels enter which path?Original, processed scene, neutral analysis, held images and immutable selections.

“Original” and “the image on screen” are different objects

Clean Original

The decoded, rotation-correct camera image, before stabilization, Extra Stable holds, viewing effects, UCT and drawing ink. It is not sensor Bayer data or the original compressed USB bitstream. New recording and RAM recap consume this clean path.

Processed scene

The image after the applicable geometry and display processing. The persistent completed scene is sampled by the viewport. Pan, zoom, Fill/Fit and resize can reuse it without advancing stateful camera effects.

UCT analysis scene

A separately owned, geometry-correct scene after orientation, stabilization and keystone, before appearance effects, display zoom and Super Resolution. Recognition coordinates stay in this full scene.

Immutable media selection

An owned selected video sample with document, track, request/generation, real PTS and native issuance identity. Pause holds that authority; another allocation or another repaint cannot turn it into another sample.

Capture, processing and presentation run on different clocks

Media Foundation acquires real camera samples. The preferred accelerated route retains GPU textures and uses D3D11/D3D12/CUDA interop; compatibility routes can use CPU pixels. Processing publishes a completed scene. The viewport can present that scene repeatedly while the user navigates, up to its selected/display-limited rate. A 30 FPS camera does not acquire 120 independent pictures because navigation refreshes at 120 Hz.

OnPresented is preflight for an attempted presentation. Only OnPresentationSucceeded records successful presentation submission. Busy slots defer a present and retain the prior successful view; they do not certify that a newer picture reached the display. Source, rotation, extent and relevant processing changes revoke incompatible text immediately.

Temporal Smooth owns a pair, not self-confirming averaged text

On CUDA, OCR can read a neutral temporal average. The same lease also owns the exact current single-frame image. Its history epoch, seed, alpha and blend count describe the average. Fingerprints, reference patches and later motion checks use current single-frame pixels. Every candidate word must also register the averaged OCR input against that current image at NCC ≥ 0.94 and visibility ≥ 0.95, with zero translation/refinement. Otherwise an old fading word could validate its own after-image.

The neutral average advances on actual processed frames, independently of the 80 ms analysis-opportunity throttle. Source/seek/history/geometry changes and alpha changes reseed it. Saved-frame replay restores pre-frame history so an unchanged held sample is not blended twice. Neighbour-only OCR does not create that temporal stack. This EMA is not a multi-frame OCR voting or super-resolution stack.

Extra Stable keeps the real acquisition identity

Extra Stable can temporarily display the last settled sharp frame during shake or focus loss. Its 32-byte acquisition record travels with the held image and is read after completion. Every repeat remains one acquisition. Previously proved words can survive only exact held-pixel validation. Starting UCT during a hold may yield reading-pane text, but a copied held frame cannot independently corroborate new glyphs. Recovery crossfades and unknown composites supply no recognition/proof authority. Clean Original recording and recap continue independently of the held view.

Two owned analysis slots protect UCT copies from overwrite. The per-slot limit is 128 MiB and at most 8192 pixels per axis; a smoothed/current pair shares that limit. Busy admission skips an opportunity. Unknown GPU completion retains the full allocation/input/output closure instead of freeing memory still in use.

03 · Display zoom is not OCR enlargementExplore the actual context, alignment, 704/480 regional policy and paused 8× limit.

Two different magnifications

Display zoom changes what part of the retained scene fills the window. Desktop zoom runs from 1× to 16× in Fill framing; Fit shows the whole scene at 1× while retaining the saved Fill zoom. OCR enlargement changes the detector’s input sampling for a chosen source crop. It is independently bounded. Enlarging blurred source pixels cannot recreate lost characters.

04 · One automatic backend chain, several job typesThe startup Windows bridge, preferred TensorRT and fallbacks are not parallel OCR votes.

One recognizer job chooses a backend

The default AutomaticRecognizer has three candidates in order: the application’s production TensorRT candidate, Paddle ONNX Runtime CPU, and built-in Windows OCR. Saved GPU detector/recognizer tiers default to medium/medium; CPU fallback defaults to tiny/small. Medium/small GPU recognizers default to batch eight, tiny to batch one. Saved choices and explicit overrides win.

TensorRT preparation runs asynchronously on its own owned preparation worker. While it prepares, the automatic chain uses Windows OCR at candidate index 2 as the startup bridge. It deliberately avoids occupying the OCR worker with slow CPU model initialization during that interval. If Windows has no usable OCR language, the bridge waits for preferred preparation; ordinary CPU/Windows fallback still applies afterwards. This startup bridge is more specific than the shorthand “CPU OCR while GPU prepares.”

Between jobs, readiness can promote the preferred GPU candidate. If it is unavailable or genuinely fails, normal full work can advance to the configured Paddle CPU pair, then Windows. Candidates initialize at most once per session and a retired candidate is not resurrected by promotion. Cancellation or a bad input does not authorize running a fallback. Turning UCT off/on or changing model configuration revokes the old worker’s authority and creates a fresh session without joining a native call on the UI thread.

Inside a Paddle read

  1. Plan whole-scene/context crops and normalize each detector input. Detection uses its pinned graph and bounded connected-region extraction.
  2. Keep four-corner text geometry. Rectify each detected line/region for recognition.
  3. Use the recognizer’s 1024 × 48 canvas by default. Very long lines can add up to eight overlapping windows; capped windows widen to retain coverage. CTC decoding reduces model output to dictionary tokens and estimated source spans.
  4. Merge duplicates/fragments carefully, retaining distinct occurrences of an identical word at different places. Group rows/columns without swallowing neighbouring type sizes.
  5. Prepare local colors, bounded source-ink fitting, scene coordinates, reference patches and candidate/omission dispositions on the worker.

The limits include at most 128 detected regions per pass, up to 1152 compact quad descriptors per pass with long-line windows, and at most 1024 merged lines. Detector thresholds are 0.2/0.4; the separate candidate confidence threshold is 0.7. These are bounded algorithms, not quality guarantees. The optional bucketed-width experiment uses separate cached 128/256/512/1024 profiles; the fixed1024 default remains available.

Windows is a real local engine, not a model bundled with OkuFlow

Windows.Media.Ocr uses installed Windows OCR language support. It selects profile languages, then an installed OCR-capable language. The adapter scales to the engine’s reported maximum, converts to grayscale and inverts a dark surrounding image when appropriate. It now maps the engine’s native text angle and sampled resize back to source quads. No account, network call or LLM is involved.

05 · Step through the producer/consumer queueOne in flight, one latest pending, full priority and retained evidence.

Slow OCR cannot grow an unbounded backlog

The controller produces work from eligible owned images; a worker consumes it. The recognition scheduler has one executing job and one replaceable pending slot. A newer ordinary job replaces waiting work. A full job can replace a waiting region; a region cannot replace a waiting full. Replacing pending work does not preempt a native call already running.

The GPU controller is stricter than the scheduler’s capacity: it does not park a pending device OCR lease while inference holds the other analysis slot. That keeps the two-slot pool available for independent validation. CPU descriptors and scheduler contract fixtures can demonstrate the pending slot; the following illustration is not a claim that every camera frame queues GPU OCR.

Read the complete sequence as text
  1. Full A starts from an owned acquisition.
  2. Full B waits while A runs.
  3. New full C replaces waiting B; there is still only one pending job.
  4. A finishes. Its result needs authority and current proof; C begins.
  5. C finishes. With an idle worker and sufficient optional slack, region D enters the waiting slot just before worker dequeue.
  6. Demanded full E replaces waiting D before dequeue.
  7. A new region F cannot replace waiting full E.
  8. E begins; a source/geometry change revokes its publication authority and cancels any waiting work.
  9. E’s late native return cannot publish into the new source; ownership retires only with known completion.
  10. An immutable selection H is read once under standalone held-frame admission.
  11. The paused app requires no neighbour job; eligible words use H itself.
  12. Pan/zoom projects the retained artifact; it does not invent another acquisition.
  13. An unchanged completed still stays idle; one useful same-image detail job is separately bounded.

There is also an independent validation consumer

The probe worker has one executing and one latest waiting request. It constructs/checks bounded scene probes without waiting for inference. It never runs an optional regional OCR descriptor. Probe work uses a 60 ms cooperative search budget, with cancellation between lines or bounded 64-line resident batches. Completed checks remain usable; unchecked new lines stay hidden. An old valid line retains only its own still-fresh proof.

Source/seek/geometry/disable changes increment authority immediately. Input storage remains owned until native work actually finishes. Scheduler destruction waits at most 1.5 seconds, then can detach an independently owned state with callbacks revoked. Cancellation is a publication/ownership fence, not a promise to interrupt any vendor instruction at an exact time.

Why read the same-looking page again?

The baseline full read remains the authority for new, missing and changed content. An unchanged-looking area may contain a new digit, a cursor, a hand, a slide edit or a small OCR error. The existing NCC tracker was designed for motion/persistence checks. Its character-change diagnostic found false accepts, so it cannot safely turn “this still correlates” into “skip recognition here.” The region planner’s repeated-word count is also only bookkeeping from full OCR.

Consequently a stable live or advancing scene still gets full refreshes, and a baseline full job can rerun detection/recognition on overlapping context containing already accepted words. Those extra crops can recover small text lost in a downscaled full view. Preparation, source checks, color/ink fitting and admission also take work. This is deliberate conservatism plus current implementation limits, not an implemented unchanged-region inference cache.

Some repeated work is already avoided: viewport movement reuses the completed scene and accepted artifact; stable scenes obey refresh scheduling; identical accepted geometry/colors can remain cached; immutable completed stills do not poll periodically; model plans are reused. Optional supplemental regions add focused work between full reads. They do not reduce the number of mandatory full reads.

What actually demands a new full read?

Initial/source/seek/geometry/processing authority changes, pending unconfirmed words, lack of accepted text, a view not covered sufficiently by the prior job, actual probe contradictions and due refresh can demand full OCR. Stable probe similarity defaults to 0.92. A repeated Extra Stable acquisition does not confirm pending text; it is reread only under the narrower lost-text/refresh recovery policy. These conditions are more specific than “OCR every N milliseconds.”

07 · How a recognized candidate earns replacement eligibilityConfidence, source size, exact text, independent images, geometry and protection.

Recognition output is a proposal

A candidate must survive both source/evidence validation and final display decisions. A high score, two agreeing OCR reads or a large accepted-word count is insufficient to prove the text is correct or painted. Failures preserve the source image and ordered omissions rather than inventing missing text.

  1. Preserve exact authority. The copied pixels carry source epoch, geometry generation, frame/acquisition sequence, source time, applied transform and pixel extent. Jobs/results/accepted lines keep that stamp. The controller never restamps old pixels as the currently displayed source. Cancellation and incompatible source/geometry changes revoke them.
  2. Require usable candidate metadata. Candidate confidence is finite, at least 0.7 and no more than the small upper tolerance 1.0001. Bounds/quads must be positive, finite and valid in their source image. Minimum word height is seven source pixels, with only 1e-5 source pixels of arithmetic tolerance. Enlarging the detector or viewport does not waive this source-size gate.
  3. Apply notation policy. Unsupported symbols, non-ASCII digits and modifier-letter notation remain original. Ordinary ASCII labels and lookalikes follow the normal checks; there is no blanket veto on letters mixed with numbers.
  4. Prepare source proof. The line reference comes from the job’s exact current single-frame pixels, even when OCR read an average. Textureless/untrackable references do not authorize glyphs. Rejected spans retain source-protection rectangles.
  5. Require temporal agreement. Ordinary live acceptance uses exact text at an overlapping scene position, with agreement IoU ≥ 0.5, from independent recognitions or an already accepted word under its valid proof. Repeated held copies do not supply another acquisition.
  6. Check current pixels. Ordinary line tracking requires NCC ≥ 0.82 within a bounded ±6-probe-pixel translation search. A newly recognized live/advancing line needs a current-frame probe at or after its OCR frame. Failed, out-of-view or unchecked new lines remain hidden. This motion tracker is distinct from strict immutable/current-input registration.
  7. Use the selected admission policy. Paused video/manual Freeze admits eligible OCR from the exact held image with its own valid source references, without temporal corroboration. The explicit two-frame protocol requires exact text, distinct complete source digests, valid geometry, NCC ≥ 0.94, visible fraction ≥ 0.95 and bounded two-probe-pixel refinement. Averaged-input/current-frame registration still uses 0.94/0.95 with zero translation/refinement.
  8. Keep display authority current. Ordinary results have a four-second arrival-age limit. Lines retain their validation clock and obey freshness. A 600 ms absence of successful presentation receipts hides glyphs and reading text; attempted presents, worker completions and toggles cannot restore them. A genuine successful receipt can restore an unchanged still-valid artifact.

Independent does not mean infallible. Two real frames can show the same obstruction, and an OCR model can make the same semantic mistake twice. Exact-text agreement and pixel registration establish consistency with those images, not the true spelling or meaning. UCT has no dictionary/LLM correction stage that can safely turn F.ight into Right.

Acquisition identity and pixel identity do separate jobs

A capture-owned acquisition record proves which actual image was held. Immutable proof additionally requires different full source content, not merely different object addresses. CPU images hash their active rows. Saved/unknown GPU input computes a domain-separated SHA-256 tree over all packed BGRA pixels on CUDA: 4096-byte leaves prefixed by 0x00, ordered groups of up to 16 child digests prefixed by 0x01. Only the 32-byte root returns. Padding is excluded. Digest kinds must match before different roots establish distinct content, so switching backend cannot manufacture independence. Live capture does not routinely hash every frame.

Proof is bounded, including on dense pages

The shared admission ceilings include 1024 lines, 8192 words and a union cap of 32768 tokens/masks in applicable merge paths. Resident reference/match requests batch at most 1024 descriptors; worker checks use 64-line chunks. Paused oriented word patches sample 8–64 columns by 4–12 rows, with margins in the word’s own detected frame. Word registration retains at least 75% of the larger intrinsic edge sizes and bounds translation to 0.25 normalized scene units per axis. These are not an expanded optical-flow motion search.

Ink fitting chooses display geometry; it need not produce identical crops in neighbouring captures. In the explicit corroborated protocol, word proof first checks fitted frames, then permits one original neighbour-bounded recognition-frame check under the same thresholds. Small tilts spanning more than half a source pixel stay oriented instead of switching fitters at an angle boundary. Current paused/Freeze admission does not run this cross-frame comparison or treat a reread as independent evidence.

Work stops conservatively when a budget or cap is reached. A completed worker check can be consumed after the UI’s inline match budget expires; unfinished checks are not silently counted valid. Unknown GPU completion is quarantined. These policies constrain application-owned work; they are not a total process RAM/VRAM ceiling or native-driver time guarantee.

Omissions own source protection

A rejected/uncertain token leaves an ordered … in reading order and protects its source ink from adjacent replacement plates. Protection can also live in a bounded mask-only carrier with no readable words. Candidate merging is transactional: cross-owner carving, appends, retirements, counters, pending evidence and provisional IDs must all succeed before commit. A late risk/cap refusal restores the prior state.

Original protection and carved subtraction remainders are explicitly distinguished. On projection, an original thin mark rounds conservatively outward if either axis has no covered pixel centre. A carved remainder does so only if both axes lack centres. Thus a harmless thin carved strip need not suppress its adjacent proved word, while original punctuation keeps protection. If identical rectangles carry both classifications, original wins. A diagonal/ambiguous quad cut is refused rather than exposing unproved corners through a rectangular approximation.

08 · Shadow planning and optional regional executionMetadata priority, unchanged full deadlines, and a no-withdrawal transaction.

The planner observes full OCR; it does not track optical flow

With no opt-in, no planner is allocated. OKUFLOW_UCT_SHADOW_PLANNER=1 enables an observation-only occurrence/grid prioritizer on the existing OCR worker. OKUFLOW_UCT_REGIONAL_OCR=1 enables the supplemental executor and implicitly the planner. Shadow alone does not run a crop or publish text.

Only successful complete full results update the planner. Each repeated spatial word occurrence has a separate ID; the same spelling in two places is two occurrences. Fresh full output pairs by geometry before comparing text. Repetition from two fresh verified acquisitions can mark it “stable,” meaning only that full OCR repeated it. Changed full text wins. Held duplicates, stale tickets, unregistered averaged text and partial results cannot advance occurrence ages or coverage debt.

The default observer retains at most 2048 occurrences, 1024 lines and 8192 words, an 8 × 8 unseen-coverage grid, and 24 hypothetical selections with six reserved for older coverage debt. Focus is a priority hint, not evidence. The plan always keeps authoritativeAction = kRunFullOcr. It is not an OCR text cache, a smooth slide/shadow detector or a stack of images.

Only spend optional slack

Active mode may submit one region per full interval when the worker is idle and no full work is demanded/pending. It reads current owned pixels from a strictly later acquisition than the retained full plan. Full deadlines, last-full markers and refresh requirements remain unchanged. The controller reserves a probe period beyond the cost estimate; the worker rechecks a steady-clock full deadline and an additional 80 ms execution reserve after metadata/readback, immediately before inference.

The initial estimate is 750 ms; later estimates use the nondecreasing maximum of the old estimate and 2 × actual regional worker duration + 80 ms. Full fallbacks do not update that regional estimate. This generally fits stable 1500 ms intervals, not demanded 350 ms intervals. Measured high-water cost cannot predict every call. An overrun is counted and disables optional work until worker recreation; baseline full work continues.

A queued full supersedes a queued region. Expired slack yields without inference; it cannot trigger a more expensive full call instead. With sufficient slack, unsupported detail or a stale/unusable plan can fall back once to full work on that same current input under the worker’s policy. A supplemental backend failure adds no CPU retry or bridge readback and does not retire/promote a healthy candidate; optional work latches off and the next current full read handles normal failover.

Crop ownership includes the detector’s complete union

The safe interior is ten source-crop pixels from internal edges: the backend’s shared eight-pixel exclusion plus two pixels of detector jitter slack. True image-border edges add no margin. These values are not divided by 704/480. Original detector bounds, fitted words and masks must all fit. Ink fitting cannot make a clipped detector fragment trustworthy.

Admission owns complete lines. A sentence, word or mask crossing/outside safe coverage stays verbatim, with its identity, reference, offsets and proof clocks. An optional crop that never observed an outside line does not require another probe merely to retain it. Its ordinary freshness and independent contradictions still apply. Rejected edge candidates contribute no pending words or temporal observations. If a rejected candidate touches an otherwise owned line, the optional update remains wholly unapplied.

“No withdrawal” includes the actual raster

A geometric rectangle comparison alone is insufficient: conservative original punctuation can share a raster column with a nearby word even when their ideal rectangles are disjoint. Optional admission therefore prepares a bounded proposed model and checks that it does not withdraw existing eligible text/protection. When new protection or changed geometry could affect an old glyph, the controller uses the production renderer in a scratch preflight.

That preflight matches old glyph identities, requires an actual current paint in the same view/style, and prepares one proposed raster plan with the same layout, exclusions, quads and protection closure. Cold typography, stale paint, exceptions or insufficient remaining budget make the optional admission a no-op. The scratch operation does not assign public content, create windows, pump events, alter visibility or publish timing. RAII restores temporary state. Model-only callers conservatively refuse novel protection while earlier glyphs exist if they cannot obtain this renderer proof.

The approximate 3 ms UI admission budget is cooperative, not a hard cap on every library call. Cached warm layouts reduce work; dense closures may yield. A refused optional update changes no public artifact, pending evidence, IDs or publication ticket. Full OCR and independent pixel probes keep their authority to correct or remove text.

Detector/CTC boxes often include padding. Bounded worker-side fitting samples local ink within the line and neighbouring-word limits. The oriented path removes connected components spanning opposite search edges, separating neighbouring rules/surfaces where possible. Its fit keeps at least 35% of detected width and 25% of padded search height, with a four-source-pixel height floor; the independent seven-pixel candidate height policy still applies to prepared words. One solid foreground and one local background per word

Local word colors can keep dark letters on a selected yellow button while adjacent words stay light on dark. Sampling comes from neutral source analysis before display LUTs; the replacement is not recolored from the final viewport. Worker preparation/cache work stays off the pan/zoom paint path.

ReadableColors uses black/white polarity for low-chroma foregrounds whose RGB channel spread is below 64. A clearly chromatic foreground is preserved only when the existing contrast reaches at least 7:1. Otherwise the foreground first moves to the polarity endpoint. Clearly colored paper (RGB channel spread at least 64) stays unchanged when that solid ink reaches a 3:1 source-color floor, preserving white-on-blue chat bubbles. This floor is not a font-size-dependent accessibility certification. On neutral paper or below that floor, the background moves the minimum distance toward its endpoint. Background brightness uses 64 levels and small same-polarity changes retain prior colors within an eight-channel-level hysteresis band.

This creates solid, readable replacement ink. It deliberately normalizes some muted source colors: RGB spreads 17–63 now go black/white too. That is a color-fidelity tradeoff, not universal denoising. Sampling near the spread-64 boundary can still vary. Raw SceneWord metadata may retain a tint such as #9bb6bf while the actual paint color is white after ReadableColors.

Only admitted replacement plates cover source pixels. Faint compressed source ghosts can remain above/below a plate or outside its fitted footprint. UCT does not denoise the whole video, erase an unproved word, or guarantee complete anti-aliasing/halo removal. Clearly colored source labels are not all forced white.

The final paint plan checks useful rendered size, valid shape/font layout, duplicates, conflicting text, chrome/viewport clipping and source-protection closure. 12 · Interface languages are not OCR language coverageModel dictionaries, installed Windows support, notation and uncalibrated confidence.

Three language questions have different answers

  1. Can I use the controls in this language? OkuFlow has 25 interface languages, with a saved explicit choice or system default. That covers labels/status/localization resources, not every OCR alphabet.
  2. Can the chosen engine emit these characters? Paddle recognition is limited by the pinned selected model and its dictionary/configuration; tiers have different coverage.   3. Will UCT admit and render the recognized token well? The candidate policy, font/shaping/layout, source size and proof gates are additional restrictions. A dictionary entry or translated interface is not validated multilingual reading quality, RTL usability or screen-reader acceptance.

When Windows has no OCR-capable language, its fallback reports that recognition is unavailable and leaves the original image. The current token policy allows ordinary ASCII labels

GPT6, GPT-6, RTX4090, OCRv6, R2D2 and isolated ASCII lookalikes such as l I 1 0 O o are not rejected just because of their shapes. They still need confidence, source size, temporal and pixel proof. The earlier blanket ASCII lookalike/mixed-label veto is not restored.

There is no reliable general handwriting, curved-page, mathematical-layout or diagram classifier. A token slipping through ordinary print checks is not evidence that those use cases work. UCT does not apply an LLM, dictionary autocorrect or semantic spell repair. “Show original” remains useful for comparing names, symbols and numbers.

Paddle’s confidence comes from its OCR decoding evidence; it is not calibrated probability that a displayed word is correct. Windows OCR does not expose a comparable confidence score in this wrapper, so words carry the nominal default 1.0, unscored. That value is not 100% semantic certainty and cannot be compared as a calibrated score with Paddle.

Independent acquisitions, exact-text agreement, source registration and conservative masks improve consistency. They do not verify spelling, meaning, a hidden character or the correctness of two repeated mistakes. Lowering the cutoff can admit more fragments; raw candidate/paint counts do not establish which words are right.

Earlier cached-plan worker timings remain descriptive receipts under their own source/device/load boundaries; they are not a fair speed comparison merely because one number is lower.

Not implemented as a safe general pipeline

  • Optical-flow reuse of recognized textNo flow field transports text authority across scenes. Ordinary bounded patch translation and oriented paused registration are different mechanisms.
  • Shadow-aware smooth slide followingThe “shadow planner” is metadata occurrence/coverage bookkeeping. It does not segment shadows, predict a slide transition or preserve text through arbitrary moving shadows.
  • Multi-frame OCR image stackingNo registered stack/voting/super-resolution OCR scheme combines several acquisitions to recover characters. Neutral EMA is implemented, with separate current-frame proof, and is not that proposal.
  • Safe unchanged-region OCR skippingThe current character verifier is not accepted for reuse. Full reads remain authoritative; active regions are supplemental.
  • General semantic correctionNo LLM/dictionary correction, occluded-character reconstruction, guaranteed math/handwriting/curved-page interpretation or calibrated word-correctness probability.
  • Complete native and release acceptanceReal-camera quality, broader devices/drivers, physical sustained recap, native NVDA/Narrator/Magnifier/UIA behavior, mixed-monitor/DPI behavior and the full combined GUI/release gates remain separate work.
