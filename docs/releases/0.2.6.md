# OkuFlow 0.2.6

## Reading and video

- Ultra Clear Text uses batched GPU crop preparation and decoding, bulk result
  transfers, and faster word merging while retaining its two-slot pipeline.
- Supported GPUs prefer BF16 independently for the selected detector and
  recognizer, with same-tier FP32 fallback for supported preparation failures.
  Engines are built and cached locally for the user's GPU. Recognition remains
  an ML result and can miss or misread text.
- Freeze and Resume retain the displayed framing and preserve eligible text
  through the handoff to advancing frames. Completion-triggered admission and
  deadline expiry reduce avoidable waiting and delayed stale-text removal.

## View and controls

- Aspect-aware zoom can show the whole picture and snaps at Fill framing.
- The bottom actions are Photo, Record, Explain and Draw.
- Assistant startup uses isolated application control configuration.

Use the MSI for installation, or extract the entire portable ZIP and open
`oku_flow.exe`. The adjacent `app` directory is required; the launcher alone is
not a portable application.

This release retains the unsigned-preview distribution channel. Windows
SmartScreen may warn about an unrecognized publisher.
